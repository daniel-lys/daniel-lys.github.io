Puzzle Play

A jigsaw puzzle for young children, cut from your own photos.

Pick a photo, choose how many pieces, and it becomes a puzzle. Everything happens on the phone: the picture is never uploaded, and every puzzle already on the device plays without an internet connection.

What it does

  • Puzzles from your own photos, or from the pictures that ship with the app.
  • Difficulty from a handful of pieces up to a full board.
  • A shelf of finished puzzles, kept only if you ask for them.
  • A corner where a grown-up can prepare puzzles in advance and leave a note at the end.
  • Seven languages: English, Romanian, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese.

Ads and privacy, briefly

The app is free and shows ads between rounds. It is registered with Google as directed to children, so those ads are not personalised and the Android advertising ID is not collected — the permission for it has been removed from the app. The privacy policy sets out exactly what is and is not collected.
